# One Piece X Launch Playbook

This is the operating guide for the two live X accounts connected to the platform.

## Account roles

### account1

Use this as the manga-first account.

- Best for chapter reactions
- Best for theories
- Best for spoiler-contained discussion
- Best for direct reader debate prompts

Suggested display name:

`One Piece Manga Daily`

Suggested bio:

`Daily One Piece manga reactions, theories, callbacks, and debate prompts. Spoilers are clearly labeled. Join the crew.`

Alternate bio:

`One Piece manga talk every day. Chapter reactions, theories, power debates, and spoiler-tagged discussion for real fans.`

Pinned post option:

`This page is for daily One Piece manga reactions, theories, and fan debates. Spoilers will always be clearly labeled. If you like chapter talk and big discussion threads, you’re in the right place.`

### account2

Use this as the anime-safe account.

- Best for episode reactions
- Best for arc callbacks
- Best for anime breakdowns
- Best for broad engagement without manga spoilers

Suggested display name:

`One Piece Anime Daily`

Suggested bio:

`Daily One Piece anime reactions, arc callbacks, episode talk, and fan questions. Anime-safe unless clearly marked.`

Alternate bio:

`One Piece anime every day. Episode reactions, favorite moments, arc callbacks, and spoiler-safe discussion for the fandom.`

Pinned post option:

`This page is for daily One Piece anime reactions, callbacks, and episode discussions. Anime-safe by default. If anything goes beyond that, it will be clearly marked.`

## Daily workflow

### Morning

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Check `Recent Activity` for any failed posts.
3. Check `Queued Posts` and remove anything outdated or weak.
4. Save the plan for today if it is not already stored.
5. Use `Save + Queue Plan` for scheduled posts you want auto-published.

### Before each major window

1. Confirm the queue contains the correct scheduled post.
2. Confirm the account tone matches the account role.
3. If there is breaking One Piece news, update the next post instead of forcing the old one live.

### Evening review

1. Check which posts actually published.
2. Note which hooks got replies, reposts, or profile visits.
3. Rework tomorrow’s weak posts before queuing them.

## Queue rules

- Manual drafts are for ideas that still need review.
- Scheduled and auto-approved posts are allowed to go out through the scheduler.
- If a queued post feels generic, remove it instead of letting it publish.
- Do not mix manga spoilers into `account2`.

## What to prioritize for growth

- Question-led hooks
- Strong opinion posts
- Callback posts tied to a current episode or chapter discussion
- Clear “pick one side” debates
- Short posts that are easy to reply to

## What to avoid

- Long setup before the hook
- Generic praise posts with no discussion angle
- Spoiler ambiguity
- Repeating the same debate framing too often
- Publishing weak filler just to hit volume
